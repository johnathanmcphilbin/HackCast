how do I log hours?
Keep a JOURNAL.md in your repo. Add an entry about every hour: what you did, how long, and a photo.
what goes in the repo?
A README, photos of the build, and all the source and design files. No live URL needed.
do I need a BOM?
Yes, unless it's only a 3D print. Name every part properly, like “Seeed Studio XIAO RP2040”.
PCBs, wiring and CAD?
Editable files: KiCad project, schematic and PCB, a wiring diagram, or STEP/F3D. Exports or STLs alone don't count.
