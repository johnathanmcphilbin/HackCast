CUE CARD · Q01
what is hackcast?
hackcast is a weekly, 25–40 minute podcast by teenagers, and a Hack Club You Ship, We Ship programme. you build projects, log your hours, earn shop rewards and get a say in what happens on the show.
CUE CARD · Q02
who can participate?
hackcast is for teenagers. full eligibility details will be posted here before launch.
CUE CARD · Q03
what can I build?
pretty much anything: websites, games, apps, hardware, tools, bots, art projects. if you made it, it counts as building.
